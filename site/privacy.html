Watch Home Privacy

Watch Home transmits only the active room identifier, Netflix watch identifier, playback position/state/rate, synchronization timestamps, sequence numbers, and a random reconnect client identifier needed to coordinate an active watch party.

It does not intentionally collect Netflix credentials, Netflix cookies, real names, email addresses, audio/video content, subtitles, advertising identifiers, analytics identifiers, unrelated browsing history, or search terms.

See the full repository privacy notice .
